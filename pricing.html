Frequently asked

What counts as a "processed minute"?

Every minute of source footage Enveral analyzes — from an uploaded file or a pasted link.

What video formats and sources work?

MP4 and MOV uploads up to 2GB, or a link to a YouTube, Google Drive, or Vimeo video.

Can I cancel anytime?

Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. Downgrade to Free whenever you like.
